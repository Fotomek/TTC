3. Server log files

When this website is accessed, the web server processes technically necessary connection data. This usually includes:

Which data is collected in detail depends on the configuration of the hosting provider. The processing serves to provide the website and to ensure the stability and security of its operation. 4. Contacting us

This website contains no contact form. If you contact us by email or by phone, we process the data you provide (e.g. name, contact details, content of the message) solely to handle your enquiry.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures, and otherwise Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries).

We store your details only for as long as is necessary to handle your enquiry and delete them afterwards, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Please note that emails sent unencrypted can be read by third parties.
