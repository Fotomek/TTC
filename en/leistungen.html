Services

Initiator and partner for digital infrastructure.

TTC Ventures supports the development of the next generation of digital infrastructure as a strategic initiator.

Services in the field of infrastructure

  • Strategic development of data center and infrastructure projects

    Identification, connection and development of projects, companies and investors along the entire value chain of modern data infrastructure.

  • Partnerships between business, technology and capital

    We bring together companies, capital, technology and public-sector stakeholders to develop viable projects with a long-term perspective.

  • New investment and business models

    The growing demand for artificial intelligence, cloud services and high-performance data centers requires new infrastructure, new partnerships and new investment models.

  • Europe’s digital sovereignty

    Europe’s digital sovereignty is among the greatest economic and technological challenges of the coming decades. We support it.

  • Sustainable and energy-efficient infrastructure

    We look at the entire ecosystem – from energy supply and grid infrastructure to operation and waste heat utilization.
