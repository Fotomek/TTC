About the Foundation

Observe. Interpret. Orient.

The Foundation is concerned with observing and analyzing economic, technological and societal developments in the context of Human Intelligence and Artificial Intelligence.

At its center are questions of relevance, credibility and strategic orientation in increasingly complex markets and communication spaces.

The Foundation sees itself as an independent platform for research, interpretation and strategic perspectives. It combines human experience, analytical market observation and AI-supported evaluation systems into an integrated research approach for entrepreneurs, executive leadership, family businesses, opinion leaders and institutional decision-makers with long-term responsibility.

Way of working

Research. Insights. Service.

  • ResearchWe observe, analyze and understand developments, trends and connections.
  • InsightsWe recognize patterns, gain insights and develop forward-looking perspectives.
  • ServiceWe share knowledge, create orientation and support people and organizations.
