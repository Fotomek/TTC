Natural Intelligence

Humanization between NI and AI. Evolutionary intelligence for people and nature.

02

Sustainability

In harmony with nature.

We act for the benefit of people and in harmony with nature. People and nature form the basis for a future worth living.

For digital infrastructure, this means: we promote sustainable and energy-efficient infrastructure – from operation through to waste heat utilization.

We develop viable projects with a long-term perspective.

03

Data Centers

Data centers are becoming the most important infrastructure projects of the 21st century.

Europe’s digital sovereignty is among the greatest economic and technological challenges of the coming decades. The growing demand for artificial intelligence, cloud services and high-performance data centers requires new infrastructure, new partnerships and new investment models.

TTC Ventures supports this development as a strategic initiator. Our focus is on identifying, connecting and developing projects, companies and investors along the entire value chain of modern data infrastructure.

We bring together companies, capital, technology and public-sector stakeholders to develop viable projects with a long-term perspective.

04

Energy Utilities

We look not only at the construction of data centers, but at the entire ecosystem.

It ranges from energy supply and grid infrastructure, through land, financing and technology, to operation, waste heat utilization and the digital applications of the future.

Energy supply and grid infrastructure stand at the beginning of this chain. Waste heat utilization stands at its end.

We bring together companies, capital, technology and public-sector stakeholders.
